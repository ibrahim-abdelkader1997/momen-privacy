About the App

Momen Prayer is a clean, offline-first Islamic utility app designed to assist with daily prayers, Qibla direction, the complete Quran with tafsir, and supplications. Completely free with zero ads.

Privacy Policy

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect or share any personal user data.
  • Offline Architecture: The app has no internet permissions; all data stays strictly on your device.
  • No Trackers or Ads: Zero third-party trackers or advertisements.

Bundled Content (No Downloads)

The complete Quran, the tafsir, the hadith, the duas and all audio recordings are bundled inside the APK. The app downloads nothing. Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings removes every value it has stored (settings, Quran bookmarks and last reading position).

  • Quran text: Tanzil Quran Text (Uthmani v1.1), Hafs from Asim — CC BY 3.0, stored verbatim and never modified.
  • Quran font: Amiri Quran — SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  • Tafsir: At-Tafsir Al-Muyassar, King Fahd Complex for Printing the Holy Quran (Madinah), via QuranEnc.com — redistributed verbatim.
  • Hadith: the fawazahmed0 hadith dataset — The Unlicense (public domain).
  • Audio: CC0 recordings by Adam-synagda and sonically_sound; one CC BY 3.0 Fajr Adhan from the Islamic Center Malmö; five Doha, Qatar Adhan recordings marked with the Public Domain Mark 1.0; a Salawat recording by Ibra7imbey via Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License; the remaining Salawat and notification chimes are original recordings made for this app.

Full attributions and complete licence texts are available offline inside the app under Settings → Sources, licenses & attributions. The in-app page also shows the Arabic and English rights notice for the Salawat recording: Momen Prayer does not claim ownership or exclusive rights to that recording, all rights remain with their respective holders, and a documented rights complaint will be reviewed and may lead to the recording being suspended, removed or replaced.

Hadith & Dua Sources

Hadith are taken only from the authentic collections (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah) with the book and hadith number shown. Duas are from the Qur'an and the authentic Sunnah. The dataset follows the open-source fawazahmed0 hadith collection (The Unlicense / public domain) and the numbering used on sunnah.com.
