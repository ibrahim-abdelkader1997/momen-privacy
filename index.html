About the App

Momen Prayer is a clean, offline-first Islamic utility app designed to assist with daily prayers, Qibla direction, and supplications. Completely free with zero ads.

Privacy Policy

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect or share any personal user data.
  • Offline Architecture: The app has no internet permissions; all data stays strictly on your device.
  • No Trackers or Ads: Zero third-party trackers or advertisements.

Security & Verification

  • VirusTotal scan report
  • MobSF static-analysis report
  • Privacy policy (online)

Hadith & Dua Sources

Hadith are taken only from the authentic collections (Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami at-Tirmidhi, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah) with the book and hadith number shown. Duas are from the Qur'an and the authentic Sunnah. The dataset follows the open-source fawazahmed0 hadith collection (CC0) and the numbering used on sunnah.com.
